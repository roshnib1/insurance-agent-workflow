3Inspector Remarks

During the site visit, exposed and frayed wiring was observed near the induction furnace feeder line in Bay-3, along with an overloaded distribution panel serving the CNC machine bank. The main panel's incoming breaker terminals showed thermal discolouration consistent with historical overheating. Circuit breaker bypassing was observed on two circuits. These findings are inconsistent with the 'no electrical hazards' disclosure made in the accompanying proposal form and warrant immediate rectification prior to policy inception.
